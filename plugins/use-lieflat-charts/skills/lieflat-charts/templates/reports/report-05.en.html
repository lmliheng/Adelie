One year of
answers in public

OPEN OFFICE HOURS started as an experiment: every question about building our product gets answered in the open — on the forum, in the changelog, or on a call anyone can join. Twelve months later the habit has outgrown the experiment. 1,204 questions were asked this year; most found their answer in public, where the next person can find it too.

Most questions arrive in the week after a release.

Release weeks run about six days and show up as spikes three to four times the quiet-week baseline. The pattern repeated after all three launches this year.

Public answers keep working after we log off.

A private reply helps one person once. A public one compounds: answered threads were revisited 11,830 times this year — almost ten reads for every original question. Forty-one answers graduated into documentation pages. If a question taught us something, it should teach everyone. That is the whole model.
